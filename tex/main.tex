\begin{abstract}
This paper investigates the algebraic and dynamical properties of generalized Collatz systems governed by $a_{k+1}\cdot\beta^{\val(a;k+1)} = \alpha\cdot a_{k} + \gamma$.
We identify periodic characteristics within the reduction sequences and construct a hierarchical model for the corresponding sets of source values.
Furthermore, we demonstrate that the algebraic structures inherent in these sets exhibit extreme rigidity; this result establishes a proportional relationship among the densities of these source-value sets.
% Leveraging these, we classify the distribution patterns of source values for a class of simple generalized Collatz systems, thereby offering measure-theoretic insights into them.
Finally, we formalize the Collatz system as a quasi derivation defined over a groupoid structure.
This formalization reveals intrinsic connections between generalized Collatz systems and classical problems such as  the ABC conjecture and the Power Difference Problem, thereby providing a dynamical systems and algebraic topology perspective on the two famous questions.
\end{abstract}
